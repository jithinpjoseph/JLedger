Welcome to Jledger

A simple offline app for two things: planning what to buy, and tracking what you actually spent. No account, no internet needed — everything stays on your phone.

📝

List — before you shop

Type an item and tap the +, or tap a suggestion chip. Check items off as you buy them. This is just a checklist — nothing here counts as money spent yet.

🧾

Ledger — after you shop

This is where money actually gets tracked. Tap the gold + button, type what you bought and how much, done. That's the only button you need here.

📊

Insights — see the bigger picture

Spending trends, totals by store and category, and monthly subscriptions all live here — check it whenever you're curious, no daily upkeep needed.
